# Paper playtest: three wakings

Status 2026-10-23. Three wake screens written as the player reads them, one seed, one run, to test on the page what `story-and-endings.md` adds (chronicle levels, dread and awe, memory tiers, prophecy, a kept historian, an artifact voice) before any of it is built. Everything below is mock text in the briefing's format (`ages-sim.md` 17.5, `core-loop.md` section 5). Read it on the phone; note where you stop reading, what you would do, and what you cannot tell from the screen.

Valley of this seed: Aldmere (human, vale) · Dorun (human, marches) · Kell Hold and Deep Hold (dwarves, hills) · Ysaril (elves, wood) · the Red Scar (orcs) · Mount Harrow (the lair). Legends at hatching told of the Breaking of the Vale 600 years before, the dwarf king who dug too deep, and an elven queen who has reigned since the Breaking.

## Waking 2 · year 138 · you slept 100 years

**Chronicle: written by Osric.** The scribe you took from Wellmere's abbey in year 12 has kept the record. He is 141 and writes slowly. The "because" lines are his.

### What changed for you

**A page from Osric's chronicle, found on your pillow.** *In the eleventh year of the war the lords of Dorun hired an elf-reader to copy a book from Ysaril's vault. It is the book you later took from Dorun's library in year 14. I have read it. It is a prophecy. I have copied the lines that concern you below and I do not like them.*

> When the mountain is loved by four peoples,
> and a house of stone stands for it in each,
> and the air is thick again with old craft,
> and one living voice speaks for it to all,
> the mountain will leave, and the stone will stay.

*The dwarves read "the mountain will leave" as the end of the world and have started a school of engineers. The priests of Aldmere read it as a promise and have started a shrine.*

**Hooks**

1. **Slayers forming.** Dorun has founded an order, the Broken Fang, after the burning of Wellmere Bridge in year 14. Their master is **Hald Ironarm**. Stage: forming. *Answer:* Kill Dorun's army at the marches, or Demand that Dorun disband them. *If ignored:* they are ready in about two wakings.
2. **A thief in the hoard.** Forty gold is missing. Osric saw a man of Kell Hold with a sack in year 101. *Answer:* Take or Kill at Kell Hold. *Pays:* the gold back and a half again. *If ignored:* Kell Hold grows rich on it and tells the story.
3. **Pilgrims.** Eleven of Aldmere's faithful stand at the foot of the mountain. *Answer:* land and receive them, or eat them. *If ignored:* the shrine cools.

**Growth.** You slept on 640 gold, 3 books, 1 artifact, the embers of Wellmere Bridge. Might +6, Flame +5, Lore +3, Arcana +1. Two talents are open:
- Flame 8: *Sea of Fire* (burns spread to neighbouring regions) | *Clinging Fire* (burned land recovers at half speed; embers ×1.5)
- Lore 3: *Old Tongues* (Demand opens at half the Lore; more envoys) | *Keen Eyes* (an envoy's lie is shown)

### While you slept

**Headline.** Aldmere won the river war, because you burned Wellmere Bridge under Dorun's host in year 14 and the host never crossed again.

- Years 14 to 31: the war ended in Aldmere's favour. *Because* Dorun's army was broken at the bridge, Dorun paid tribute to Aldmere for sixty years.
- Year 44: Queen Maelis died. Her grandson Teodric reigns. He keeps the oath his grandmother swore to you; he has never seen you.
- Year 66: Dorun built castles on every hill. *Because* the burning is written in their chronicle and taught to every knight, they have not forgotten.
- Year 90: Dorun founded the Order of the Broken Fang. *Because* of the burning, and *because* the tribute to Aldmere ended and the lords needed a cause.
- Year 101: a thief from Kell Hold took gold from the hoard. *Because* Kell Hold has had no dealings with you and does not fear you.
- Year 120: a shrine to the mountain was raised in Aldmere. *Because* of the oath kept for a hundred years, and the prophecy.

## Waking 3 · year 620 · you slept 300 years

**Chronicle: sources in the hoard.** Osric died in year 402. His grandson Wil kept the book until 470 in a worse hand. After that there is nothing of yours, only what the courts wrote, and they do not agree.

### What changed for you

**Two accounts of the same century.**

*From the Chronicle of Dorun (taken from its library in year 140):* In the year 410 Master Hald Ironarm of the Broken Fang went up the mountain with twelve knights and came down alone and burned. He lives still by the grace of the wards. The dragon sleeps, and the engines are ready.

*From the Book of the Shrine (gift of Aldmere, year 300):* In the year 410 the mountain woke in its sleep and the murderers of Dorun were judged by fire. One was spared to carry the word. The dragon's temple was begun that year.

Osric's book would have told you which is true. The sim knows. You do not.

**A voice from the hoard.** The iron crown you took from the drowned king's vault in year 320 speaks when you are alone: *"They are building cannon. The living will always build cannon. I can make you something cannon cannot reach."* → `commune` (ask · accept · refuse)

**Hooks**

1. **Slayers at the gate.** The Broken Fang is **ready**. Hald Ironarm, 230 years old by some alchemy, burned on the left side, wears a ward-cloak since 410 that halves your fire on him. Year of the attempt if you sleep: within 40 years. *Answer:* fight him at the lair's border. *Pays:* his artifact, dread in every court. *If ignored:* 31 % that you die in your next sleep.
2. **A rival god.** In Aldmere the shrine became a temple and the temple split: the Order of the Flame says the dragon is the prophecy, the Reformed say the dragon is its heresy. The Reformed hold the capital. *Answer:* burn the Reformed temple, or consecrate the Order's, or judge the Reformed high priest. *If ignored:* the Order is driven out within a generation.
3. **A door in the temple.** Ysaril's queen has begun a rite. Portal progress 80 %. *Answer:* none needed; this is an exit, if you want it, in about two wakings. *If ignored:* the door may open without you.

**Growth.** You slept on 2,900 gold, 9 books, 4 artifacts, 2 royals (both now dead of age in the lair; their lines are dragon-raised in Aldmere and Kell Hold), 1 great work of elven art. Might +14, Lore +9, Arcana +6, Dominion +4. Talents open:
- Arcana 15: *Dragon-sight* (every defence's range and damage shown; forecast twice as sharp) | *Hex* (a curse also halves the court's anti-dragon progress)
- Dominion 15: *Terror* (dread ×1.3, decays slower) | *Gilded Throne* (the gold bed gives +25 % Might)

### While you slept

**Headline.** The Broken Fang went up the mountain in 410 and came down alone. *Cause unknown to you.* (Wil's book stops at 470; the two chronicles above disagree.)

- Year 330: Dorun's engineers reached bolt-throwers. *Cause (Dorun's chronicle):* the fear of the burning.
- Year 402: Osric died at 405 years, the oldest man who ever lived. *Cause (Wil):* the hoard kept him; it did not keep him young.
- Year 410: the Fang's attempt. (Above.)
- Year 470: Wil's book ends.
- Year 480 to 610: *no account of yours.* The courts' books mention: a plague in Dorun; the elves of Ysaril withdrawing from the border woods; Kell Hold selling bolt-throwers to Dorun; the temple schism in Aldmere.

## What to tell me

1. Where did you stop reading on each screen?
2. Waking 1 at level 0: is "no causes" interesting (a mystery) or just empty?
3. Waking 2: is the "because" line what makes the world feel reactive, or the hook that names your own act?
4. Waking 3: do the two contradicting accounts work, or do you just want the truth?
5. The sleep table: can you read the bet from it? Is the slaying row enough, or do you need the research milestones per length?
6. Which single thing on any screen would you act on first?
7. Dread and awe as words (low, mid, high) or as bars or numbers?

What the mock cannot show: the montage during sleep, the map, the sound of the crown speaking. It also assumes the historian's "because" lines come from `Event.cause` (`ages-sim.md` 17.1), which exists, and that chronicle level is a filter on which causes the briefing is allowed to print, which does not exist yet and is the smallest piece of the whole story layer to build.
